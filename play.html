AirHockey with Copper

Meet Copper, your new rink rival. Quick hands, a bouncing puck, and first to seven. Think you’ve got him?

How to play: drag your orange paddle around your half. Mouse works too; arrow keys or WASD on desktop. Score through the goal mouth. First to 7 wins.

Full-screen rink, sound off by default. Pause anytime. Exit with ✕ or Esc. Plays offline after the app has cached this update.

Your record stays on this device. Copper is an original Copper & Blue News character — no team logos or official mascots.
