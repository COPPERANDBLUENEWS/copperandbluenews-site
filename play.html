    How to play: drag your orange paddle around your half. Mouse works too; arrow keys or WASD on desktop. On touch, the paddle stays slightly above your finger. Score through the goal mouth. Against Copper, first to 7 wins. Online is two minutes. More goals when the clock hits 0:00 wins. A tie goes to sudden-death overtime, next goal, with a one-minute cap, then it is a tie. You each sit at the bottom of your own screen.

    Full-screen rink. Your goals sound an oil-field horn, and the puck clacks off paddles and boards. Mute turns all of that off and stays on this device. Pause anytime. Exit with ✕ or Esc. Plays offline after the app has cached this update.

    Your Copper record stays on this device. Online play uses a username, a secret stored in this browser, and a recovery code you can save for another device. Email comes later. Copper is an original Copper & Blue News character — no team logos or official mascots. Privacy
