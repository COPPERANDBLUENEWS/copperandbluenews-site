    How to play: ten questions, fifteen seconds each. Both players in a duel get the same set. A correct answer is 100 points, plus up to 50 for the time you have left, plus 15 for each correct answer already on your streak (five steps, so 75 at most). A miss scores 0 and the streak starts over. The higher total wins the duel. A tie stays a tie. Practice is there whenever nobody is waiting in Quick duel.

    Quick duel looks for another player for a few seconds. A room code is a direct challenge, the same idea as AirHockey. The server keeps the clock and the answer key.

    Trivia uses its own username, saved on this device, plus a recovery code for another phone or browser. It is a separate account from AirHockey, with the same kind of sign-up. Points, duel record, accuracy, and your best streak stay with that name. We don’t store your raw IP address. No Oilers or NHL logos. Privacy · AirHockey
