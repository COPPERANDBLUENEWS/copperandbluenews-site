Gameday tomorrow: Rangers at Oilers

Gameday tomorrow: Rangers at Oilers

The Oilers are 12-3-3 with 27 points and sit 1st in the Pacific after Saturday’s 4-2 win over Vancouver. The Rangers are 10-7-1 with 21 points, 3rd in the Metro, and come into Rogers Place on a three-game win streak.

The special teams battle is a strength against a strength. Edmonton’s power play is clicking at 33.8% (22-for-65), and New York’s penalty kill is elite at 89.1% (49-for-55). The Oilers score 4.17 goals a game; the Rangers score 3.06 and allow 2.89.

McDavid has a point in all 18 games this season, with 10 goals and 37 assists for 47 points. Draisaitl is tied for the NHL goal lead with 16, and Bouchard has 31 points. For the Rangers, watch Fox (22 points), Lafrenière (18) and Zibanejad (15).

Dylan Garand will likely start in net for New York, since Shesterkin is out for the season with a lower-body injury. Edmonton hasn’t said whether Levi (7-1-0) or Jarry (4-1-3) gets the start.

Keep an eye on Nugent-Hopkins. He left Saturday’s game after taking a puck to the face and didn’t come back. Babcock doesn’t expect him to miss much time, but his status for Monday is still unclear.

The Oilers won the first meeting 4-1 at Madison Square Garden on Nov 1. Now it’s the rematch at Rogers Place.

Monday, Nov 9 · 7 PM MT | 6 PM PT · Rogers Place · SN+
