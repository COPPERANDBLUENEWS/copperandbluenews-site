GAME DAY 🔶 Oilers at Maple Leafs, Scotiabank Arena, 6 PM MT / 5 PM PT.

Edmonton (14-3-3, 1st in the Pacific) opens a six-game road trip chasing a fourth straight win. McDavid has a point in all 20 games to start the season, and Toronto (8-11-2) has lost five straight. Revenge spot: the Leafs won 7-2 in Edmonton on Oct. 24.
