GAME DAY: Canucks @ Oilers

GAME DAY: Canucks @ Oilers 🛢️

Two straight losses (3-4 SO in New Jersey, 5-4 vs Anaheim). Time to get back on track. Edmonton (11-3-3) still sits 1st in the Pacific, and in comes Vancouver: 3-14-1, last in the league, four straight losses.

McDavid’s start is ridiculous: 44 points (10G-34A) in 17 games, with an assist in every one of them. Draisaitl has 14 goals, and Bouchard has 30 points from the back end.

Tale of the tape: the Oilers score 4.12 goals a game (most in the NHL) to Vancouver’s 2.94. The power play is cashing at 33.9% vs the Canucks’ 21.4%, and Vancouver gives up 4.61 a game.

What to watch: the crease. Andersen and Levi are on IR and Jarry is day-to-day with an illness, so Matt Tomkins is the projected starter against Leevi Meriläinen. Edmonton is 9-0-2 when it scores first, so a fast start matters. The last meeting was a 9-7 Oilers win in Vancouver on Oct 1.

Puck drop 8:00 PM PT at Rogers Place. Sportsnet.

Will the Oil get back in the win column?
