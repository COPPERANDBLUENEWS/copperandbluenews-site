TIED AFTER THREE.

TIED AFTER THREE.

Travis Sanheim tied it at 11:42 of the third, a wrist shot from Rasmus Ristolainen and Matvei Michkov, and it’s 2–2 after regulation at Rogers Place. Overtime is next.

Zach Hyman scored twice in the second: a snap at 4:13 from Mattias Ekholm and Colton Dach, then a power-play backhand at 14:03 from Connor McDavid and Evan Bouchard. Tyson Foerster got the Flyers on the board with a power-play wrist at 16:49 of the second, from Owen Tippett and Christian Dvorak.

Tristan Jarry has 30 saves on 32 shots.
