OILERS WIN 3-1. DRAISAITL SCORES TWICE, LEVI STOPS 28.

Leon Draisaitl scored twice and Devon Levi turned aside 28 of 29 shots as Edmonton beat the New York Rangers 3-1 tonight at Rogers Place.

Connor McDavid opened the scoring unassisted at 6:42 of the first, his 11th of the season. Draisaitl made it 2-0 at 2:59 of the second (Vasily Podkolzin, Mattias Ekholm), his 17th. Jaroslav Chmelar cut it to 2-1 with a shorthanded wrist shot at 5:05, assisted by Mika Zibanejad. Draisaitl sealed it with an unassisted, shorthanded empty-netter at 18:47 of the third, his 18th.

Levi, the first star, finished 28-for-29 (.966). He was perfect at even strength (18/18) and on the penalty kill (10/10). Dylan Garand stopped 17 of 19 in the loss.

The Rangers outshot Edmonton 29-20 and won 54% of the faceoffs. The Oilers led in hits 23-19 and blocks 11-7. Neither power play scored (New York 0-for-5, Edmonton 0-for-3). Edmonton has won both meetings this season, including a 4-1 win in New York on Nov. 1.

The Oilers are 13-3-3 (29 points), first in the Pacific, and have won two straight. They’re back at Rogers Place on Wednesday, Nov. 11, against the Philadelphia Flyers, with puck drop at 7 p.m. PT 6 p.m MT
