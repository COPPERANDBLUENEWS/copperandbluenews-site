GAME DAY Oilers at Panthers, Amerant Bank Arena, 6:00 PM MT / 5:00 PM PT.

GAME DAY 🔶 Oilers at Panthers, Amerant Bank Arena, 6:00 PM MT / 5:00 PM PT.

Edmonton (16-3-3, 1st in the NHL) is chasing a sixth straight win in the third game of a six-game road trip. The Oilers are 9-0-1 away from home, still without a regulation loss on the road. McDavid has a point in all 22 games to start the season (12-41—53), tied with Dany Heatley (2005-06) for the fifth-longest season-opening point streak in NHL history. Draisaitl is the league’s first 20-goal scorer (20-21—41).

Florida (12-7-3) holds the 2nd wild-card spot in the East, is 5-2-0 at home, and is coming off a 2-1 overtime loss in Dallas. It’s the first meeting of the season and a rematch of the 2024 and 2025 Stanley Cup Finals.

Nugent-Hopkins is questionable (undisclosed) after sitting out Sunday in Philadelphia. These PROJECTED lines assume he’s out, with Dach at 2C and Hallander in:
