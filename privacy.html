Privacy

The Copper & Blue News is a fan-run Edmonton Oilers site. Reading the site needs no account and we don't use ad trackers or cookies. This page covers the Game alerts (web push notifications), the game day email newsletter, the first-goal poll and the installable app.

What we collect

Only if you turn on Game alerts:

  • Push subscription: the address and keys your browser creates so it can receive notifications for this site. It doesn't contain your name, phone number or location.
  • Alert preferences: which alerts you picked (game day morning, 1 hour to puck drop, 15 minutes to puck drop, lineup confirmed, puck drop, goals, period ends, final score, and the optional betting odds).
  • Name or email (optional): only if you type one in. Leave it blank and alerts work the same.
  • The date you signed up or changed your choices.

Newsletter sign-up is separate (see below). Your alert choices are also saved in your own browser (local storage) so the panel remembers them.

Why

To send the Oilers game alerts you asked for. If you add a name or email, it's only so The Copper & Blue News knows who signed up and can contact you about the alerts. We don't sell or share it, and we don't send marketing email from this form.

Game day email newsletter

Only if you separately tick the unchecked consent box and submit the newsletter form, we collect your email to send game day previews and Oilers news from The Copper & Blue News. The form states: “Game day previews and Oilers news from The Copper & Blue News. Unsubscribe anytime.” We record the consent timestamp, wording/version and the page/form used. Entering an optional email for push alerts does not subscribe you to the newsletter.

We never sell or share your email with advertisers or other publishers. Cloudflare stores the list on our behalf as our infrastructure provider. We do not put newsletter emails in browser storage, public pages or site analytics. A short-lived, non-reversible keyed identifier is used at the edge to limit repeated sign-up attempts; raw IP addresses are not saved in newsletter records.

Unsubscribe anytime using the signed, no-login unsubscribe link in a future newsletter, or message @copperandbluenews to ask us to remove your email before newsletters begin. Unsubscribing removes your email and consent record from the active newsletter list; it does not change your push alerts. We keep your newsletter sign-up only until you unsubscribe or ask for deletion. No email is sent by the sign-up form.

Where it's stored

Sign-ups are stored in a database run by The Copper & Blue News on Cloudflare. Notifications are delivered by your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla), which only sees the encrypted alert. The app's offline copy of pages lives on your device.

The bell opens Notifications, a list of alerts the site has sent (1 hour and 15 minute reminders, puck drop, goals, period ends, finals, confirmed starting goalies and lineups). That list is the text of the alert, not who received it. It has no name, email or push subscription. Which ones you have opened is saved only in this browser (local storage) and is not sent to us.

Game day poll

On a game day the home page can ask who you think scores the Oilers' first goal. If you vote, this browser creates a random id (saved only on your device) and we store that id with your pick for that game. It isn't your name, email, or account. Picks are deleted automatically after about 120 days. Clearing this site's data in your browser forgets the id; it doesn't delete the pick already stored.

AirHockey online

If you play AirHockey online, you pick a username. We store that name, your wins, losses, and a rating, plus a hash of a random device secret and a hash of a recovery code. The secret itself stays in this browser (local storage) so someone else can’t take the name. The recovery code is shown once so you can sign in on another device; signing in that way replaces the previous device secret. There is no email on the account yet — the record has a place for one later. We don’t store your raw IP address; a short-lived hash is used only to limit sign-ups and new matches. Message @copperandbluenews if you want the username deleted.

Site analytics

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It's cookieless: it doesn't set cookies, use local storage or fingerprint your device, and it doesn't follow you to other sites. We only see aggregated totals, such as page views, which pages are popular, the site that linked you here and your country. We can't tell who you are from it. About Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Unsubscribe or delete

  • Tap the bell at the top left of any page, then Alert settings, then Unsubscribe. That turns alerts off and deletes your sign-up (subscription, preferences and name/email) from our list.
  • Or block notifications for copperandbluenews.com in your browser or phone settings. The next alert we try to send fails and your sign-up is deleted automatically.
  • To ask for deletion or a copy of what we hold, message @copperandbluenews on Instagram.
