Privacy

The Copper & Blue News is a fan-run Edmonton Oilers site. Reading the site needs no account and we don't use ad trackers. This page covers the Game alerts (web push notifications) and the installable app.

What we collect

Only if you turn on Game alerts:

  • Push subscription: the address and keys your browser creates so it can receive notifications for this site. It doesn't contain your name, phone number or location.
  • Alert preferences: which alerts you picked (puck drop, goals, period ends, final score, lineup confirmed).
  • Name or email (optional): only if you type one in. Leave it blank and alerts work the same.
  • The date you signed up or changed your choices.

We never ask for anything else. Your alert choices are also saved in your own browser (local storage) so the panel remembers them.

Why

To send the Oilers game alerts you asked for. If you add a name or email, it's only so The Copper & Blue News knows who signed up and can contact you about the alerts. We don't sell or share it, and we don't send marketing email from this form.

Where it's stored

Sign-ups are stored in a database run by The Copper & Blue News on Cloudflare. Notifications are delivered by your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla), which only sees the encrypted alert. The app's offline copy of pages lives on your device.

Unsubscribe or delete

  • Tap the bell at the top of any page, then Unsubscribe. That turns alerts off and deletes your sign-up (subscription, preferences and name/email) from our list.
  • Or block notifications for copperandbluenews.com in your browser or phone settings. The next alert we try to send fails and your sign-up is deleted automatically.
  • To ask for deletion or a copy of what we hold, message @thecopperandbluenews on Instagram.

Not affiliated with the Edmonton Oilers or the NHL.
